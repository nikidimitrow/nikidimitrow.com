Background

From Safety-Critical Automotive to Field Edge AI

Two seemingly separate worlds—subsurface exploration and resilient aerial autonomy—share the exact same engineering backbone: deterministic embedded systems operating on noisy physical sensors where failure is not an option.

15+ Years ISO 26262 QNX / Hypervisors

Automotive Safety-Critical Architecture

Over 15 years architecting production automotive systems under strict functional safety (ISO 26262), real-time operating systems (QNX), hypervisor-isolated domains, and formal verification workflows. That discipline—timing budgets, fault containment, and verifiable behavior—is what most AI prototypes lack when moving into the field.

Academic & Domain Bridge AIUT MGU

Control Engineering & Geosciences

Grounded in Automation, Information, and Control Engineering (AIUT) and connected to the University of Mining and Geology (MGU), bridging classical control theory and custom hardware with modern Geo AI and dual-use edge perception.

Why these two niches? Because both Geo AI and dual-use defense sensing require on-device intelligence under harsh, connectivity-denied, safety-critical conditions. Solving sensor synchronization, uncertainty quantification, and constrained compute once creates leverage across both subsurface exploration and airspace awareness.
