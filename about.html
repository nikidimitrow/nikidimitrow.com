About

I'm Nikolay (Niki) Dimitrow, a Principal Software and Systems Architect based in Sofia, Bulgaria.

For 15+ years I have built safety-critical embedded systems for the automotive industry: cockpit domain controllers, hypervisor architectures, QNX, Android Automotive, Adaptive AUTOSAR and multi-supplier integration under ISO 26262. I also work close to the metal: CPU microarchitecture, RTL, and formal verification with Yosys, SymbiYosys, Verilator and cocotb.

Why Geo AI

Most exploration decisions are made with expensive information: drill holes, lab assays, full surveys. Most of the value is in deciding which measurement to buy next. That is a sequential decision problem under uncertainty, the kind of problem I have spent my career making reliable.

I'm pursuing this at the University of Mining and Geology "St. Ivan Rilski" in Sofia, where I am completing a degree in Automation, Information and Control Engineering, with master's studies in Exploration Geophysics and Mining Electrical Engineering planned next. The university gives me what the internet can't: laboratories, instruments, field work, rock samples and people who have spent decades underground.

Why edge AI and dual-use

The same pipeline (a cheap sensor, an on-device model, a trustworthy decision) finds an ore body or an unexploded shell. A drone-borne magnetometer does not know which one it is looking for. I build for both, openly where possible and responsibly where not.

How I work

  • In public. Datasets on Hugging Face, code on GitHub, preprints on arXiv.
  • On real hardware. Every model is measured where it will run.
  • With safety discipline. Requirements, failure modes and evidence, not just accuracy.
