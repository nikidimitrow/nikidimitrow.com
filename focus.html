Technical Focus

Where Field Sensing Meets Safety-Critical Compute

I deliberately focus on two complementary domains—avoiding broad generalism in favor of deep systems engineering where sensor physics, uncertainty quantification, and embedded execution meet.

Domain 01 Geo AI

Geo AI & Subsurface Decisions

Geological exploration is fundamentally a sequential decision problem under high uncertainty and high cost per sample.

  • Sequential Decision-Making: Adaptive sampling strategies that update subsurface belief models in real time.
  • Bayesian Optimization: Gaussian Process surrogates and expected-improvement / entropy-search policies for optimal drill-hole placement.
  • Archive Digitization: Structuring legacy National Geofond logs, maps, and borehole records into ML-ready datasets.
  • Field Geophysics: Edge signal processing for UAV magnetometry and GPR surveys.
Domain 02 Dual-Use Edge AI

Dual-Use & Resilient Edge AI

Field autonomy requires deterministic perception and sensor fusion directly on constrained hardware, without assuming reliable connectivity.

  • Aerial Perception: Quantized (INT8 / FP16) vision models benchmarked on Jetson Orin Nano for real-time aerial recognition.
  • Sensor Fusion: Tightly coupled IMU, GNSS-denied, optical, and geophysical data streams with time-synchronized logging.
  • Resilient Embedded Systems: Partitioned hypervisor/RTOS architectures (QNX, Linux RT) isolating safety-critical control from AI inference.
  • Verification: Deterministic timing budgets, watchdog supervision, and formal/runtime safety monitors.
Dual-Use & Compliance Policy: My work focuses on sensing, perception, resilient embedded architectures, and open benchmarks. I publish methods, architectures, and reproducible performance metrics—never weapon guidance, lethality systems, or technologies controlled under EU Dual-Use Regulation (2021/821).
